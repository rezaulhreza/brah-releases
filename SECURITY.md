# Security policy

Found a security weakness in Brah, its website or the licence system? Email **brah@ferrolo.com** with "Security" in the subject line. Do not open a public issue.

The full policy, with what is in scope, the rules for research and the safe harbour, is at **https://brah.ferrolo.com/security**.

Problems in the AI services you connect to Brah (OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, Deepgram, ElevenLabs, your own Hermes server or local model), or in services Brah uses (Stripe, Apple, GitHub, Cloudflare, Vercel), go to the company or project that runs them.
